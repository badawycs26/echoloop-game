Echo Loop: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Echo Loop app ("the app") uses. The app is published by Marwan Badawy ("we", "us"). Contact: Badawycs@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server run by us. We do not collect your name, email or phone number through the app.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows rewarded video ads. Our advertising partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID and some technical data to show ads.
  • The app is not directed to children.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, scores, hints, settings, daily streak) is saved in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Progress does not move to a new phone.

3. Advertising

The app is free and is supported by optional rewarded video ads. To show ads, the app uses an ad network SDK:

  • AppLovin MAX (AppLovin Corporation), and
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC), only if and when we add it to the app.

These providers, acting on their own behalf, may collect and use: your advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, and information about ads shown and interactions with them. They use this to deliver, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalize ads. We do not receive your personal data from these providers.

Their policies: AppLovin Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your phone's settings (Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads). Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app where this option is provided.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device. The reminder is created locally by the app and does not use a server. You can turn it off in the app settings or in your phone settings.

5. Feedback

If you use "Send feedback", the app prepares a message (your rating, your comment, the app version, level and device type) and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. The only sharing happens through the ad providers described in section 3.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Security and retention

We do not keep your data on any server. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. Data held by ad providers is governed by their own policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if we add a new ad provider or a feature that uses more data. The date at the top shows the latest update.
