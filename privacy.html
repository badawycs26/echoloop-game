Echo Loop: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Echo Loop does not collect, store or send any personal data to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases in this version.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, scores, daily streak, hints left) and your settings (sound, vibration, theme, reminder) are saved only on your device. They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Share button

The "Share result" and "Send feedback" buttons only open your device's share sheet with a short text. You choose whether and where to send it. We do not see it unless you send it to us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. It is optional, and you can turn it off in Settings or in your device settings. No notification data leaves your device.

Network

The game runs offline from files inside the app. It does not send information about you or your device to us or to third parties. Links such as this policy open in your browser.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if ads are added in a future update. The date at the top shows the latest update.
