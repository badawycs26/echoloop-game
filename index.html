Echo Loop

Record a run. Your echo replays it while you play the next one. Plan, loop and reach the exit.

Coming soon to Android

How it works

  • Move through a small grid and press Record echo.
  • Your echo repeats your exact moves, in step with you.
  • Echoes hold pressure plates so doors stay open for you.
  • Reach the glowing exit in as few moves as you can.

What you get

  • 50 handmade levels across five chapters, from gentle to tricky
  • A new daily puzzle and an endless mode
  • One-way tiles and pushable crates
  • Hints, stars and scores for every level
  • Several color themes and color-blind friendly marks
  • Works offline. No account needed.

Free, no ads

The game is free, with no ads, no purchases and no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. Read the privacy policy for details.
